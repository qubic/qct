## Context — two-proposal structure

The goal is a **sustainable finance structure** for QCT: predictable monthly spending, no repeat of the June 2026 shortfall, and no risk that user-facing services stop if any single quarterly vote fails.

Given the CCF treasury is approaching its lower operating range, QCT is submitting **two proposals in parallel** for Q4 2026 to make this concrete:

1. **[Base Services Reserve](./2026-09-15-Base-Services-Reserve-proposal.md)** — 190B QUBIC. Locks in a minimum baseline for **Oct 2026 – Dec 2027** (15 months). Covers running the public apps (Explorer, Wallet, Proposals, Docs), RPC/API, archiver, basic test net + test nodes, all hosting/DDoS/domains/e-mail/monitoring, plus basic infrastructure maintenance.
2. **This proposal (Q4 2026 operational delta)** — 485B QUBIC. Adds the full Core, Integration, Testing, Management, and Client/Frontend teams **above** the base for the 3 Q4 months only.

If both pass, QCT operates at full team velocity through Q4. If only the base passes, the Main Net and its public apps stay online, but active development, advanced testing, and community operations pause.
